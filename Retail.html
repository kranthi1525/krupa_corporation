Organized Retail

Transforming unorganized kirana stores into modern, organized small-format retail outlets through structured supply chains.

5321 Supply Chain Experiment

Organizing Kirana Stores Into a Modern Retail Network

Krupa Retail is initiating the 5321 Supply Chain Experiment, an innovative approach aimed at helping unorganized kirana stores transition into modern, organized small-format retail outlets.

This initiative focuses on empowering these stores with access to better procurement options for FMCG products at competitive prices, while introducing an alternative consumer sales channel — enhancing operational efficiency and laying the groundwork for scalable franchise models.

The 5321 Framework

Wholesale Access, Reliable Supply, Digital Reach

The 5321 framework enables kirana stores to operate more efficiently by offering wholesale procurement channels for FMCG products — ensuring better pricing, reliable supplies, and a diverse product range to compete in a digital marketplace.

A robust wholesale channel provides direct access to high-quality FMCG products through bulk purchases, while a complementary consumer sales channel opens up e-commerce opportunities and enhances profitability for local retailers.

Future Vision

Preparing for Krupa Homes Concept Stores

One of the long-term objectives of the 5321 Supply Chain Experiment is to lay the groundwork for Krupa Homes — large-format retail outlets specializing in tiles, sanitaryware, electricals, and other construction materials.

By establishing a strong wholesale channel, partnering with distributors, and leveraging franchise management expertise, Krupa Retail is setting the stage for a fully optimized supply chain and an integrated digital sales platform from day one.

Integrated System

A Foundation for Growth

The 5321 Supply Chain Experiment is designed as an integrated system that combines wholesale channels, franchise management, and consumer sales into a cohesive retail ecosystem.

This interconnected approach enables Krupa Retail to address the unique challenges faced by kirana stores while preparing for larger ventures like Krupa Homes.
