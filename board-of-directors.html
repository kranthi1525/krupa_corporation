Founder's Message

Building something meaningful for India

"Our goal has never been just to build a business — it's to build something that leaves the industry, and the people in it, better than we found them."

When we started Krupa Corporation, we saw a clear gap in how construction materials were sourced, priced, and delivered across India. Traditional supply chains were opaque, inefficient, and unfair to the businesses that depended on them the most.

Our answer was Krupa Market — a platform built on transparency, powered by technology, and grounded in a genuine respect for every retailer, distributor, and builder we serve. The idea was simple: if we can make procurement faster, fairer, and more reliable, we can unlock enormous value for the entire ecosystem.

Today, with 500+ retailers supported and 20+ brand partners on board, we're just getting started. Our vision extends beyond commerce — we want to be a company that Andhra Pradesh, and India, is proud of. One that invests in communities, embraces sustainability, and proves that doing business responsibly is not a constraint, but a competitive advantage.

To our partners, customers, and team: thank you for believing in what we're building. The best chapters are still ahead.

Pradeep Devarakonda Founder & Managing Director, Krupa Corporation

What drives us

Principles we lead by

Every decision at Krupa Corporation traces back to the same foundation our founder laid on day one.

Trust First

Every partnership is built on transparency and a long-term view — not a single transaction.

Move Fast, Stay Responsible

Speed without accountability is recklessness. We move quickly, but never at the expense of our values.

Sustainability by Design

Environmental responsibility isn't a add-on — it's baked into how we build our supply chain.

People Over Process

We invest in the people who make this ecosystem work — our team, our partners, our communities.
